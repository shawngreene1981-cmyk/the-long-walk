# RESEARCH — Synchronoptic Lanes Sourcing Pass (2026-11-25)

*Companion to DESIGN_Synchronoptic_Lanes.md. Bracketed numbers refer to the Sources list. "Not verified this pass" = placeholder until checked. Several citations are Wikipedia pages that cite peer-reviewed work; upgrade before publication if academic-only sourcing is required.*

## SUMMARY

- **Ussher's Flood is 2348 BC (AM 1656)** — inside Egypt's Old Kingdom and Early Dynastic/Akkadian Mesopotamia, both keeping continuous king-lists.
- **Biblical reckonings scatter from 3761 (Jewish Anno Mundi) to 5509 BC (Byzantine)**; Septuagint vs Masoretic genealogies differ by 1,466 years.
- **Kali Yuga epoch, 18 Feb 3102 BC**: a theoretical planetary conjunction that "could not be observed" (Britannica); attested from Āryabhaṭa onward ("classical Indian astronomers").
- **Long Count zero 11 Aug 3114 BCE (GMT 584,283)**, GMT confirmed by radiocarbon (Kennett et al. 2013). Earliest carved Long Count dates 36 and 32 BCE. Other correlations would move 3114 by 100–260 years.
- **Egypt's First Dynasty (Aha's accession) radiocarbon-dated 3111–3045 BCE** (Dee et al. 2013).
- **Sumerian King List**: 8 antediluvian kings, 5 cities, 241,200 years; "Then the flood swept over"; oldest version copied under Shulgi (2084–2037 BCE); Weld-Blundell Prism c. 1800 BCE.
- **Manetho**: gods 13,900 years, demigods 1,255 … total 24,900 "lunar years" (Eusebius); totals vary by recension.
- **China**: Yellow Emperor traditionally 2698/2697 BCE (a god later historicised); first year-precise date 841 BCE; earliest writing Wu Ding oracle bones 1254–1197 BCE.
- **Göbekli Tepe c. 9600–8200 BCE** — ~5,600 years before Ussher's Creation, ~6,500 before the Maya/Kali epochs.
- **Adams chart design precedent**: streams end at conquest, split at independence, merge at absorption; question marks flag disputed dates.

**Open items before build:** Erlitou radiocarbon range; whether the Turin King List opens with divine rulers; kalpa = 1,000 or 2,000 mahāyugas; Three Sovereigns dates; the 1900 BCE Mature Harappan end; Roman Empire 27 BCE / 476 CE; Chavín revised phasing; Minoan sequence start.

## PART A: Six tradition lanes

### A1. Biblical: Ussher chronology as drawn by Adams (1871)

Ussher, *Annales Veteris Testamenti* (1650) [1][2]. Adams's 1871 chart, 23 feet, begins at 4004 BC following Ussher [3]. Clock start: Creation 4004 BC, 23 October (Julian) [1].

| Event (Ussher) | Date | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Creation | 4004 BC | [1][4] |
| Flood | 2348 BC (AM 1656); some summaries 2349 | [1][4] |
| Birth / Call of Abram | 1996 / 1921 BC | [4] |
| Exodus | 1491 BC (AM 2513) | [4][5] |
| Solomon's Temple founded / dedicated | 1012 / 1004 BC | [1][4] |
| First Temple destroyed | 588 BC | [1] |
| Birth of Christ | 5 BC | [1] |

Other Biblical reckonings: Jewish Anno Mundi 3761 BCE [6][7]; Masoretic as Ussher used it c. 4000 BC [1][7]; Septuagint c. 5500 BCE [7]; Byzantine World Era 5509 BC [7]; Alexandrian Era 5493 BCE [7].

Handover: Merneptah stele c. 1207 BCE, first mention of Israel outside the Bible [8]; Exodus date contested [8]; Ussher's 588 vs modern 587–586 for Jerusalem's fall [1][9].

**Label:** "Ussher chronology (1650), as charted by Adams 1871. A Biblical reckoning; other Biblical traditions date Creation from c. 3761 to c. 5509 BC."

### A2. Hindu: the yuga cycle

Kali era epoch 18 Feb 3102 BC (Old Style), a theoretical conjunction; "could not be observed" [10]. Fleet (*JRAS* 1911): *Sūrya-Siddhānta*, Āryabhaṭa sunrise vs midnight schools [11]. Tradition ties it to the end of the Mahābhārata war [10][12].

| Yuga | Length |
|---|---|
| Kṛta/Satya | 1,728,000 |
| Tretā | 1,296,000 |
| Dvāpara | 864,000 |
| Kali (from 3102 BCE) | 432,000 |
| Mahāyuga | 4,320,000 |

Āryabhaṭa: four equal yugas of 1,080,000 [10][12]. Kalpa 1,000 vs 2,000 mahāyugas — reconcile before drawing. Mahābhārata war dates: 3102 (tradition), c. 1000–950, 836 (B. B. Lal), c. 750–350 (Parpola); Witzel: Kuru setting c. 1200–800 [13] — contested.

**Label:** "Kali Yuga epoch, 3102 BCE. An astronomical reckoning computed by classical Indian astronomers (attested from Āryabhaṭa onward)."

### A3. Maya: the Long Count

13.0.0.0.0 4 Ajaw 8 Kumk'u = 11 Aug 3114 BCE, GMT 584,283 [14][15]. Alternatives: Mod. Thompson/Lounsbury 584,284/584,285 (1–2 days); Spinden 489,384 (~260 years earlier); Böhm 622,261 (~100 years later) [14]. Kennett et al. 2013 supports GMT [14][15]. Earliest monuments: Chiapa de Corzo Stela 2 (36 BCE, uncertain); Tres Zapotes Stela C (32 BCE); El Baúl Stela 1 (11–37 CE); Tikal Stela 29 (292 CE) first unequivocally Maya [14].

**Label:** "Maya Long Count creation date, 3114 BCE (GMT correlation). The calendar's own zero point, first carved c. 1st century BCE."

### A4. Chinese

Three Sovereigns → Five Emperors → Xia, Shang, Zhou [16]. Yellow Emperor trad. 2698/2697 BCE [16][17]; "originated as a god who was later represented as a historical person" [16].

| Milestone | Traditional | XSZ Project |
|---|---|---|
| Xia begins | 2205 BCE | c. 2070 |
| Shang begins | 1766 | c. 1600 |
| Pan Geng to Yin | — | c. 1300 |
| Zhou conquest | 1122; 1027 (Bamboo Annals) | 1046 (Cambridge 1045) |

Project contested [18]. Handover: Wu Ding oracle bones 1254–1197 BCE [19]; year-precise from Gonghe 841 BCE [18].

**Label:** "Traditional Chinese chronology (Shiji, Han-era and later computations). Year-precise from 841 BCE."

### A5. Egyptian

Manetho, 3rd c. BCE, survives in quotation [20][21]. Eusebius: gods 13,900; demigods 1,255; other kings 1,817; 30 Memphite 1,790; 10 Thinite 350; spirits/demigods 5,813; total 24,900 "lunar years" [20]; Syncellus gods 11,985 [20]. Palermo Stone [22]; Turin King List (divine rulers at its opening — to verify) [21]; Abydos list, 76 kings from Menes [23]. Bronk Ramsey 2010: Djoser 2691–2625; New Kingdom 1570–1544 [24]; Dee 2013: Aha 3111–3045 BCE [26][27]; Britannica Dyn 1 c. 2900 [28]. Unification c. 3100–2900 BCE.

**Label:** "Egyptian king-list tradition (Old Kingdom annals to Manetho, 3rd c. BCE). Reigns of gods and demigods precede the human dynasties."

### A6. Sumerian King List

16+ manuscripts [29]; Ur III copy under Shulgi (2084–2037); Weld-Blundell Prism c. 1800 BCE [30]. ETCSL [31]: "After the kingship descended from heaven, the kingship was in Eridug"; Alulim 28,800; Alaljar 36,000; "5 cities, 8 kings … 241,200 years"; "Then the flood swept over … the kingship was in Kish"; Gilgamesh 126 years.

| Chronology | Hammurabi | Fall of Babylon |
|---|---|---|
| High | 1848–1806 | 1651 |
| **Middle** | **1792–1750** | **1595** |
| Low | 1728–1686 | 1531 |
| Ultra-low | 1696–1654 | 1499 |

Year-accurate Assyrian dates from 911 BCE (763 eclipse) [32].

**Label:** "Sumerian King List (composed c. 21st c. BCE or earlier; Weld-Blundell copy c. 1800 BCE). A Mesopotamian royal tradition with reigns before and after the Flood."

## PART C: Simultaneity alignments

1. Pyramid age c. 2600–2450: Khufu, Stonehenge sarsens c. 2500 [53], Caral, Mature Harappan, Early Dynastic Sumer (solid).
2. Three beginnings 4004 / 3114 / 3102 — tradition; shared-event reading: no verified evidence. Same window as Aha (3111–3045) — coincidence.
3. Ussher's Flood (2348) vs continuous Egyptian and Mesopotamian record — solid divergence; present respectfully.
4. Two king lists, two floods: SKL under Shulgi; Manetho later places gods before kings — documents solid; single Flood no verified evidence.
5. Hammurabi's century c. 1800–1750 (contested ±60–100).
6. 13th-century horizon c. 1250–1200: oracle bones, Mycenaean destructions, Merneptah stele, San Lorenzo (solid).
7. "The world becomes datable," 911–753 (solid).
8. Axial-age empires 550–221 (solid).
9. Long Count to stone with Augustus and the Han (solid).
10. Before all six clocks: Göbekli Tepe c. 9600–8200 BCE [54] (solid).
